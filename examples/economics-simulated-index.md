---

# Level and growth are different

$$
g_t=\frac{I_t-I_{t-1}}{I_{t-1}}\times100
$$

An index level describes a position relative to a base. Growth describes a change between periods.

---

# Plot honestly

- Use a clearly labeled time axis.
- Explain any transformation.
- Distinguish nominal and real values.
- Do not infer causation from a trend alone.

---
